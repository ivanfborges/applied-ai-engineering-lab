# References

- Judea Pearl (2010), [An Introduction to Causal Inference](https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r354-corrected-reprint.pdf).
  Author-hosted corrected reprint. Section 3.3.1 defines the original backdoor
  criterion and adjustment formula; the paper separates structural assumptions
  from statistical associations and discusses mediation.
- Robin Evans, [Graphical Models, Chapter 6: Directed Graphical Models](https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/~evans/gms/_book/dag.html).
  Author's Oxford course text: DAG factorization, Markov properties and the
  ancestral moral-graph characterization of separation. An alternative to the
  explicit path enumeration used for teaching here.

Both sources were consulted on 2026-11-12. No public dataset is used: all
observations, latent variables and paired potential outcomes are generated by
the explicitly declared SCM in [example.py](example.py).
