# References

## Correlation documentation

- [SciPy: `scipy.stats.pearsonr`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.pearsonr.html)
  — Pearson coefficient, hypothesis test, warnings, and assumptions.
- [SciPy: `scipy.stats.spearmanr`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.spearmanr.html)
  — Spearman rank-order correlation and treatment of ranks and missing values.
- [SciPy: `scipy.stats.kendalltau`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.kendalltau.html)
  — Kendall tau-b/tau-c definitions and tie handling.
- [NumPy: `numpy.linalg.lstsq`](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/generated/numpy.linalg.lstsq.html)
  — least-squares solver used by the educational implementation.

  *Biometrika*, 82(4), 669–688.
- Hernán, M. A., & Robins, J. M. (2020).
  [Causal Inference: What If](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/wp-content/uploads/sites/1268/2024/04/hernanrobins_WhatIf_26apr24.pdf).
  Chapman & Hall/CRC. The authors provide a free online edition.
- Rubin, D. B. (1974).
  [Estimating causal effects of treatments in randomized and nonrandomized studies](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0037350).
  *Journal of Educational Psychology*, 66(5), 688–701.
- Cole, S. R., Platt, R. W., Schisterman, E. F., et al. (2010).
  [Illustrating bias due to conditioning on a collider](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyp334).
  *International Journal of Epidemiology*, 39(2), 417–420.

## Scope

These sources support the definitions and reasoning framework. They do not
validate the synthetic equations in this folder as a model of any real product,
user population, or AI system.
