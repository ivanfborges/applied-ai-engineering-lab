# References

## Documentation and guidance

- [SciPy: `scipy.stats.ttest_rel`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.ttest_rel.html)
  — paired t-test API, alternatives, and result fields.
- [SciPy: `scipy.stats.permutation_test`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.permutation_test.html)
  — permutation types, alternatives, exact enumeration, and randomized
  p-value conventions.
- [NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook: Tests of Means](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda35.htm)
  — reference material for common mean-testing procedures.
- [statsmodels: Power and sample-size calculations](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/stats.html#power-and-sample-size-calculations)
  — implementations for power analysis across common tests.
- [Streamlit: `st.plotly_chart`](https://docs.streamlit.io/develop/api-reference/charts/st.plotly_chart)
  — embedding interactive Plotly figures in Streamlit applications.
- [Plotly: 3D surface plots](https://plotly.com/python/3d-surface-plots/)
  — interactive surface rendering and hover configuration.

## Interpretation and reproducibility

- Wasserstein, R. L., & Lazar, N. A. (2016).
  [The ASA Statement on p-Values: Context, Process, and Purpose](https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2016.1154108).
- Wasserstein, R. L., Schirm, A. L., & Lazar, N. A. (2019).
  [Moving to a World Beyond "p < 0.05"](https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.2019.1583913).
- Phipson, B., & Smyth, G. K. (2010).
  [Permutation P-values Should Never Be Zero](https://doi.org/10.2202/1544-6115.1585).

## Scope

These references explain statistical procedures and interpretation. They do
not validate the sampling design, metric, experimental unit, or deployment
decision for a particular AI system.
